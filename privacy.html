Privacy Policy

ByteWhatever Labs develops self-hosted software projects including Family Hub.

Family Hub may connect to third-party services such as Google Calendar when explicitly configured by the user.

Google Calendar access is read-only. Authentication credentials and OAuth tokens are stored locally on the user's Family Hub installation and are not stored by ByteWhatever Labs.

ByteWhatever Labs does not operate a central service that collects or stores Family Hub calendar data.
